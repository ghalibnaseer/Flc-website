Privacy

Who we are

This website is operated by FLC & Associates LLP, Ali Street, New North Gulgasht Colony, Multan. Questions about this notice may be sent to info@flcassociates.com.

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, we receive the name, email address, telephone number (if given), area of law and message that you enter. If you email us, we receive your email and its contents.

This website does not use cookies, advertising or visitor tracking. The company that hosts the website may record technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes.

How we use it

  • to reply to your enquiry and to consider whether the firm can act for you;
  • to check for conflicts of interest before accepting instructions; and
  • to keep a record of enquiries received.

We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Enquiries are delivered to the firm’s email account and kept in the firm’s own records. They are seen only by partners and staff of the firm who need them to deal with your enquiry.

How long we keep it

If the firm does not act for you, your enquiry is deleted one week after it is received. If the firm does act for you, it becomes part of the client file and is kept with the firm’s records of the matter.

Your choices

You may ask to see the details we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete them, by writing to info@flcassociates.com.
